Implementation Services

Three ways to make your system work for you.

Reports held up by chasing updates. Data scattered across spreadsheets and emails. Schedules, risks and actions that are hard to see together. Start with the way your business works, then choose the tools and processes that help your team keep control.

01 / Improve what you have

Optimize existing systems

Make better use of Excel, Smartsheet, Microsoft Project and the tools your team already uses. Review data flows, simplify reporting, and improve schedule updates, risk follow-up and action ownership. Agree practical changes around your existing processes and licences.

02 / Adapt a proven starting point

Customize PM Tools solutions

Explore adapting Project Scheduler, BuildTrack or Risk Health Check to your workflow, reporting needs and terminology. The assessment establishes what is feasible and defines the scope. Existing PM Tools software remains licensed; customization does not transfer ownership of the underlying product.

03 / Build around your business

Commission a tailored, client-owned application

When existing tools do not fit, commission an application around your agreed requirements. The written agreement will define the bespoke deliverables and source code you own, the transfer conditions, and the handover. Any pre-existing components and third-party software will be identified separately with their licence terms; they are not included in the IP transfer.

How we work

Understand. Adapt. Put it to work.

1. Review the current operation

Discuss the reporting burden, current tools, data sources and the schedule, risk and action visibility your team needs.

2. Agree the right approach

Identify whether improving your existing setup, customizing a PM Tools solution or building a tailored application is appropriate. Define the scope, deliverables and private quotation.

3. Configure around real work

Implement the agreed workflows, calculations and reporting using representative examples, with review points to check that the setup supports the intended decisions.

4. Agree the handover

Define documentation, team guidance, ownership and any ongoing support in the scope, so responsibilities are clear after delivery.
