Questions

Before you buy.

What do I need to open the files?

Microsoft Excel 2016 or later (Excel 365 recommended) and Microsoft Word. The guide is also included as a PDF. The workbooks open in Google Sheets and LibreOffice, but sheet protection and some formatting work best in Excel.

Is it only for FIDIC contracts?

No. The examples use the FIDIC 2017 Red Book because it is widely used, but the notice and response periods are set on each Setup sheet, so you can match your own contract.

Can I change the templates?

Yes. Inputs are open everywhere. In the four control workbooks the formula cells are protected so they are not overwritten by accident; the rest of the kit is fully editable.

What is the Hartwell Commercial Centre?

A fictional USD 4.25 million commercial building used as the worked example in every file. It shows a month of real-looking project control, with numbers that reconcile across the whole kit.

Is there a Spanish version?

The templates are in English. The website and support are bilingual, and you can reply to the delivery email in Spanish.

Can I use it on client projects?

Yes, on your own projects and for the clients you work for. You cannot resell, share or upload the files elsewhere.
